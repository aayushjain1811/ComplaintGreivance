Complaint Resolution Process

Simple 4-Step Process to Address Your Grievances

1
Submit Complaint

Register your grievance online with all necessary details. Receive a unique Complaint ID via SMS/email.

2
Review & Assignment

The portal routes your complaint to the correct department. An officer is assigned for verification.

3
Action Taken

The concerned department takes necessary steps. You can track every status update in real-time.

4
Resolution & Closure

Issue resolved and you are notified. You can reopen the complaint if not satisfied.
