How It Works

Complaint Resolution Process

A simple, transparent 4-step journey from grievance to resolution

1
Submit Complaint

Register your grievance online with all necessary details. Receive a unique Complaint ID via SMS/email immediately.

2
Review & Assignment

The portal routes your complaint to the correct department. A dedicated officer is assigned for verification.

3
Action Taken

The concerned department takes necessary steps. Track every status update in real-time with SMS notifications.

4
Resolution & Closure

Issue resolved and you are notified. You may reopen the complaint if not satisfied with the resolution.
